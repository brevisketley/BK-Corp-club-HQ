# BK Corp Club YouTube Skill Adoption

Source reviewed: https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/chatgpt-youtube-agent-skill
License: MIT (source repository states MIT; preserve its LICENSE when redistributing code).
Purpose: incorporate the source's eleven focused YouTube workflows into BK Corp Club's existing channel-production process without creating a second, conflicting publishing system.

## Skill modules to adopt
- yt-script: hook-led scripts; score the opening before production.
- yt-package: evaluate title and thumbnail as one click-through package.
- yt-viral: research niche videos using channel-relative lift, not raw channel size.
- yt-retention: use retention exports to identify exact drop-off points.
- yt-edit: transcript-to-edit decision list; flag dead air.
- yt-plan: capacity-aware weekly plan (one anchor, one low-cost piece, three Shorts derived from the anchor).
- yt-comment: draft replies in the established BK voice; human review before posting.
- yt-seo: title, description, tags and search intent.
- yt-chapters: transcript-derived chapter markers.
- yt-shorts: identify short-form clips from long-form source footage.
- yt-audit: channel audit grounded in channel evidence.

## BK Corp Club operating overrides
1. Brand: BK Corp Club; premium cinematic dark-tech/robotic visual identity, consistent logo/branding and professional sound.
2. Commercial objective: qualified traffic to https://www.bkcorpclub.com and the approved MQL5 product pages; track clicks and attributable purchases.
3. Use educational, proof-led trading content. Never guarantee returns, invent results, present backtests as live results, fabricate testimonials, or imply automation removes trading risk.
4. Every description includes the website CTA and a clear trading-risk disclosure. Product links/prices must be read from approved product configuration.
5. Prefer free workflows and preserve vidIQ credit limits. Use analytics/keyword tools only when the expected value justifies the credit cost.
6. No publishing, public comment replies, thumbnail changes, or video metadata/privacy changes without approval of the exact asset and final values. These skills prepare and QA; they do not publish.
7. Do not assume a daily upload cadence. Build a realistic weekly schedule around available finished footage and actual channel analytics.
8. The existing BK channel voice and visual standards take precedence over generic templates. Do not invent a voice profile; derive it from supplied BK videos when available.
